Welcome to Saahil Kapoor's bio ™

Saahil is a rising senior at Syracuse University's iSchool, studying Information Management and Technology with a focus on Information Security and Cloud Computing. His interest lies at the intersection of tech and people, which is what's pulled him toward sales. He's spent the last several internship cycles as an SDR, consistently landing above quota results, and he currently holds a part time role with Redrob (an AI sales startup) booking meetings with university decision-makers. Long term, he's using tech sales as a launchpad toward venture capital, where he can pair go-to-market intuition with an eye for early stage opportunity.

Outside the classroom, Saahil has built a reputation as someone who gets close to the tools he sells. As Syracuse's inaugural Clay Campus Ambassador and Replit Campus Lead, he doesn't just represent these platforms, he uses them, from running Clay powered candidate searches to build out a founding member list for a campus Clay club, to experimenting with Replit for quick builds. He also serves on the executive board of his fraternity, Sigma Alpha Mu, which has sharpened how he manages relationships and organizes people toward a shared goal.

When he's not working, Saahil is usually on a golf course or a padel court, both games that reward patience and precision, much like sales itself.
He also makes time to DJ, lift, and run, and he chases new perspectives through travel; spending his second semester junior year abroad in London reshaped how he thinks about markets and people outside the U.S. That mix of curiosity, competitiveness, and hands-on experimentation is what he brings to every room he walks into.
